The Manchesters present a high-octane love letter to the legendary Fleetwood Mac, packed to the brim with timeless hits and the infamous 'Rumours' that created them.

Rachel and Peter Manchester tell the amazing story of the band — from their early years with Peter Green, through the record-breaking album *Rumours*, and on to their most recent dramas — with great humour, audience interaction, and a song list that can't be beaten. Rather than simply imitating the singers, they take the audience on a journey through the riveting story of the band and the evolution of its music, keeping the room involved the whole way through.

Experience the beautiful harmonies, intense onstage chemistry, and brilliant songwriting that turned interpersonal drama into musical gold. Come for the music, stay for the myths, and leave singing the soundtrack of a generation.

## Staging

Available with professionally created backing tracks or band charts for a full band. The show runs with a backdrop of beautiful illustrations to immerse the audience in the story. A tech rider for lighting and sound is available on request.
