Tribute Mad is a tribute show with a difference: a hilarious comedy tribute show in which two performers take on many roles, covering many different tribute acts.

The premise is simple. The rest of the twenty-person cast hasn't been able to make it, so it's down to just these two. Luckily, Rachel and Peter Manchester have incredible vocal ranges and are incredibly versatile, covering everyone from Frankie Valli and the Four Seasons to Taylor Swift and Ed Sheeran.

Along the way, expect joyous song medleys, audience participation, hilarious jokes, lightning-quick costume changes, and face-melting instrumental solos.

## Staging

Available with professionally created backing tracks. The show runs with a backdrop of illustrations. A tech rider for lighting and sound is available on request.
