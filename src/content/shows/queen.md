The Manchesters deliver an explosive tribute to the iconic legacy of Queen, belting out the stadium-sized anthems and ambitious rock-operatics that defined their legacy.

Rather than a simple tribute, Peter and Rachel Manchester channel their ultimate showmanship and captivating narrative on stage to resurrect the thrilling journey of rock's most daring and majestic visionaries — delving into the wild stories and royal antics that made Queen global icons.

Relive the breathtaking vocal arrangements, legendary stadium energy, and fearless songwriting that transformed theatrical risks into global masterpieces. This production offers a masterfully staged, non-stop ovation to a band that ruled the world.

## Staging

Available with professionally created backing tracks or band charts for a full band. The show runs with beautifully crafted screens, photos, album covers, and video footage that perfectly complement each story. A tech rider for lighting and sound is available on request.
